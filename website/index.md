features:
  - title: Share and collect
    details: Create an agenda, share the link or QR code, and everyone adds their own topics. No sign-up, people are recognised by a personal code.
  - title: Rich topics
    details: Markdown descriptions, images, polls, thumbs-up votes, comments with reactions and PDF attachments. Pin, sort or hand topics over to someone else.
  - title: Live meeting mode
    details: Roll call, a timer per topic, decisions and a Markdown protocol. All devices follow along in real time.
  - title: Everything at a glance
    details: Date, place with map, menu and a cut-off for new topics. Collapsible sections remember how you like them.
  - title: Follow-up in one click
    details: Start the next agenda with the same people and all pinned topics, straight from the end of a meeting.
  - title: Installable and offline-ready
    details: A PWA for phones and desktops. Changes made offline are queued and synced when you are back online.
